About us

An extension of your team — not another vendor.

Serendevity is a custom software development company made up of highly-qualified, talented and honestly a little nerdy IT professionals, each with their own area of expertise. Combined, we cover the full range of modern software work — from fresh startup ideas to complex, long-lived systems.

We work like an extension of your company: a small senior team that augments your own, with the redundancy and depth you need to keep the applications that keep your business running. An individual can only excel in so many things; a team has specialists in front-end, back-end, databases, cloud, design and mobile — and the habits to make it all hold together.

Serendevity (ser·en·dev·i·ty | sər-ən-ˈdi-və-tē) — a wordplay on serendipity: luck that takes the form of finding valuable or pleasant things that were not looked for.

Our promise in one cheesy-but-true sentence: the happy accident of software that just works, for years.

Our philosophy

Good. Cheap. Fast.

Every project is a triangle. You get to pick two sides, and we'll be honest about the third — because “we can do all three” usually means “we'll be late and it'll cost more.” Honesty up front is what makes the rest of the project feel lucky.

Go ahead, pick any two — it's culturally required:

Pick two and we'll plan accordingly. We'll tell you which corner you chose to skip, what it means in practice, and how to change your mind later without any drama.

Our process

How we work

Five steps, no jargon, and you'll always know where things stand. If a step feels like a meeting that could have been an email, tell us — that's literally feedback we're built for.

1

Listen

We start with questions, not answers — who uses this, what breaks today, what would make your life quietly better. We take the “why” as seriously as the “what”.

2

Map

A short, readable plan: scope, phases, risks and a timeline in plain language — the kind you can forward to a colleague without translating.

3

Build

Small iterations, working software every couple of weeks, demos you can click — not slideware. Stories stay small; surprises stay small.

4

Ship

Deployed, monitored, documented and handed over with the keys. Launch day is a Tuesday with a checklist, not a leap of faith.

5

Support

Software is a garden, not a photo. We stay on speed dial for fixes, improvements and the occasional “is this normal?” — an actual human answers.
